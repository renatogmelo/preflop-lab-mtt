## Findings and corrections

- Strongest prior element: every lesson already linked theory, a real matrix, a quiz and a trainer.
- Main gap: most chapters still showed one static context. Phase 2 adds a real interactive position-evolution lab to RFI and stack/position evolution in Explorer.
- Language remains beginner-readable while Advanced/Professional explanation layers expose range construction, blockers, domination, realization and boundary logic.
- Academy progress is completion evidence, not strategic mastery; strategic mastery continues to come from training samples.

## Remaining work

- Add dedicated interactive sequences to 3-bet, short-stack and squeeze chapters.
- Add prerequisite enforcement after enough real user data exists.
- Calibrate chapter assessments with a verified or published curated reference dataset.
